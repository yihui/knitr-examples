The function \texttt{kable()} is a simple table generator. Unlike
sophisticated packages (e.g. \textbf{xtable}, which has more than 30
arguments), this function only has a minimal number of arguments to generate
simple tables.

\begin{kframe}
\begin{alltt}
\hlkwd{library}\hldef{(knitr)}
\hlkwd{kable}\hldef{(}\hlkwd{head}\hldef{(iris))}
\end{alltt}
\end{kframe}
\begin{tabular}{r|r|r|r|l}
\hline
Sepal.Length & Sepal.Width & Petal.Length & Petal.Width & Species\\
\hline

\hline
4.9 & 3.0 & 1.4 & 0.2 & setosa\\

\section{Alignment}

By default, numeric columns are right-aligned, and other columns are
left-aligned.

\begin{kframe}
\begin{alltt}
\hlkwd{kable}\hldef{(}\hlkwd{head}\hldef{(iris),} \hlkwc{align} \hldef{=} \hlkwd{c}\hldef{(}\hlsng{"l"}\hldef{,} \hlsng{"c"}\hldef{,} \hlsng{"r"}\hldef{,} \hlsng{"l"}\hldef{,} \hlsng{"r"}\hldef{))}
\end{alltt}
\end{kframe}
